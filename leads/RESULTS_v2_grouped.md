### 4a. Mechanism: self-distillation test (Table D)

The v2 delta of +0.0631 (full feature set, seed 1) was compared with a
self-distillation control (`+distill`) that adds no synthetic rows: RF1
(the train-fitted CounsellorLabelModel) produces `predict_proba` on the
same train rows and RF2 trains on the 688 hard rows plus those soft
probes. If `+distill` reproduced the v2 delta, the gain would be label
smoothing rather than added information.

| Comparison | Delta macro F1 | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| +v2 vs real-only | +0.0631 | augmentation total effect |
| +distill vs real-only | -0.1273 | distillation-only effect |
| +v2 vs +distill | +0.1904 | difference between the two arms |

Delta(+v2) minus delta(+distill) = +0.1904 (descriptive band 0.02; no CI).
Tag: AUGMENTATION EFFECT SURVIVES.

> The control does not reproduce the v2 delta within the band. That does not by itself show that synthetic features add information, because the control is not like-for-like with +v2.
>
> **Caveat.** The control differs from +v2 in more than the presence of
> synthetic rows: (i) 4 x 688 rows with sample weights instead of 1000
> fresh bootstrap rows with sampled hard labels; (ii) its soft labels are
> RF1's *in-sample* probabilities; (iii) `class_weight="balanced"` is
> recomputed on the expanded label vector, changing the effective class
> weighting; (iv) it has no engagement variation. The no-engagement rows
> of Table E are the cleaner test of whether synthetic *profiles* help.

## 6. Limitations of this evaluation

- The real holdout has about 12 Hot rows, so every Hot F1 rests on ~12
  positives; a single prediction moves it by 0.05-0.1.
- The bootstrap CIs resample the holdout *predictions* only: they capture
  test-sampling variance, not training variance or split variance.
- Seeds re-split the same rows, so holdouts overlap across seeds; the v2
  design was iterated against the seed-42 holdout. There is no final
  untouched test set.
- The +distill control is not like-for-like with +v2 (see 4a).
- In the `full` set real rows have engagement = 0 and synthetic rows > 0.
- Lead-level leakage is removed by the grouped split; compare with `leads/RESULTS_v2.md` for the row-level numbers.
